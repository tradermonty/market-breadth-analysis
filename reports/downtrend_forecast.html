Methodology & Caveats

  • Extended sample: 12 actual corrections from 2007–2025 (up from 7 in the original 2016–2026 analysis). Two breadth-divergence episodes (price rose despite breadth downtrend) excluded.
  • Z-score normalization: Features (200MA Δ, 8MA Δ, price Δ, starting breadth levels) are standardized before computing Euclidean distance. This prevents any single feature from dominating similarity.
  • Closest analog shifted: #1 2007-07 (pre-GFC, 18.6% weight) replaced #5 2021-09 (6.6% weight) as the top match. The gradual early-stage decline is most similar to the 2007 pattern.
  • Bimodal pattern confirmed: With n=12, MEDIUM episodes dropped from 29% to 17%. Corrections tend to be either SHORT (< 90d) or LONG (> 180d) — rarely in between.
  • 8MA thresholds: Warning zone (< 40%) = elevated stress; Typical bottom zone (20–30%) = historical trough readings. Current 49.5% remains above both.
  • Time units: All scenario timelines use calendar days. Trajectory charts use trading days (x-axis labeled).
  • Caveats: n=12 is an improvement but still statistically limited. Probabilities are directional guideposts, not precise forecasts. Past patterns do not guarantee future outcomes.
